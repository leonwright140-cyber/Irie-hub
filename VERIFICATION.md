# Sprint 001 — Increment 1: Lead Management

## Acceptance criteria
- Create a lead in under one minute.
- Assign a permanent CBR ID.
- Search by name, phone, email, address, service, or CBR ID.
- Filter by status and priority.
- Move a lead through controlled states.
- Convert a lead to a customer and begin an estimate without re-entering contact data.
- Record audit events and retain data after refresh.

## Manual verification
1. Create a test lead.
2. Refresh and confirm it remains.
3. Search by phone and CBR ID.
4. Advance New → Contacted → Qualified.
5. Convert to Estimate.
6. Confirm customer appears in CRM and estimate customer is selected.
7. Export a backup.
